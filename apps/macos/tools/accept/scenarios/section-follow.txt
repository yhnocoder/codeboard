# 成员卡拖到 Section 下边外、按住不放：框在拖动中就要跟着撑开（VS Code 是这样），松手后留在 Section 里
zoom 1
center @sboxes+350,100
move @cyellow 300
rec drag
down
move @cyellow+65,150 500
wait 300
shot holding @sboxes+-20,-30 760 300
# 拖动中：框已经包住卡
expect sboxes.bottom >= cyellow.bottom
up
wait 600
shot released @sboxes+-20,-30 760 300
stop
expect cyellow.section = sboxes
expect sboxes.bottom >= cyellow.bottom

# 选中一个词，⌘K 打开链接框，输入地址，再点 Note 里别处：链接写回 Markdown（不点链接本身）
# storage: storage-tasks
# mac: demo
zoom 1
center @ntask"值"
dbl @ntask"Last line"
wait 600
rec link
dbl @ntask"scheduler"
wait 400
key cmd+k
wait 500
shot linkbox
type https://example.com/notes
wait 300
shot typed
click @ntask"Last line">
wait 600
shot after
stop
key esc
wait 800
source @ntask
